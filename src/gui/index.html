Playbook

Capture clean, performant cities.

  • Use the Satellite tile theme (Settings → Map Theme) to confirm shoreline accuracy.
  • World Scale 0.8–1.2 keeps builds recognizable while ensuring reasonable chunk counts.
  • Enable city ground for dense downtowns, disable it for rural landscapes.
  • Plan spawn point markers in the map before launching generation.
